# Tools and equipment

Wondering what type of tools we have available at reMade?
Here is a short breakdown of the type of tools we have:

## Woodworking
- power drills
- impact driver
- drill press
- hand saw
- circular saw
- table saw
- bandsaw
- electric sanders
- lather
- planer
- rasps, chisels, etc.

## Metalworking
- stick welder
- gasless MIG welder
- angle grinders
- protection equipment

## Electronics
- soldering iron
- heat gun
- multi-meter
- oscilloscope
- function generator
- bench power supply
- micro controllers
- and lots of electronic components


## 3D Printing
- we have older generation FDM 3D printers
- an honesty box for shared filament

## Other
- laser engraver/cutter
- motorcycle repair tools

We do not have provisions to do ceramics, pottery or resin printing.
